About

A one man band project, born between the streets and the stages of Bologna.

Portrait photo
assets/images/portrait.jpg

Mocked Monkey is the independent, one man band project of Alessandro Falchi, singer-songwriter from Bologna. After years of collaborations in other projects and a period of study, writing, and experimentation on stages and on the street, he completed the album Marasma in 2025.

The project was born on the street, where the audience doesn't buy a ticket, and when someone chooses to stop, they truly listen.

In a world full of noise, choosing to listen is an act of love.
