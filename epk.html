Short bio

Mocked Monkey is the one man band project of Alessandro Falchi, indie/alt rock singer-songwriter from Bologna. In 2025 he released the concept album Marasma, a record about absence, escape, and listening. Born on the street, the project carries that same spirit to every stage.

  • 01 Genre: Indie / Alt Rock
  • 02 Lineup: solo (one man band)
  • 03 Based in: Bologna, Italy
  • 04 Latest release: Marasma (2025)
  • 05 Booking: mockedmonkey@proton.me
