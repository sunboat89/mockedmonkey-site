Concept album, 2025

Marasma

A record about absence, escape, and listening: to others, and to yourself.

Listen on Bandcamp Follow on Bandcamp

The concept

The escape, the noise, the listening

The protagonist runs from everyone and everything, no longer listening to anyone, not even to himself, or to the voices in his own head, which grow louder as the story unfolds. The escape pushes him close to self destruction.

Only at the end does he understand that running was never the answer, and he chooses to embrace his own nature, finally learning to listen to himself.

The project was born on the street, where the audience doesn't buy a ticket, and when someone chooses to stop, they truly listen. In a world full of noise, choosing to listen is an act of love.
